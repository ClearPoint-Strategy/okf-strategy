## Description

It is paramount that our city government is accountable to our citizens. To be accountable, we must be responsive to our citizen's needs and always put them first.

## Status

- **Period:** Dec-22 (2022-12-31)
- **Status:** On Target

### Analysis

Our citizens feel our government is predominantly accountable and responsive to their needs. However, we need to focus on demonstrating the value our citizens receive for their tax dollars. Unfortunately, we are behind our target for that particular measure.

### Recommendations

We need to focus efforts on explaining to our citizens where their tax dollars are being spent. Ideally, that will help them understand our current tax rate and why it is set where it is.
