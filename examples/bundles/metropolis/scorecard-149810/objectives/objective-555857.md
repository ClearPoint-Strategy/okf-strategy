## Description

How our citizens navigate and get around our city is vital. Promoting an environment where travel is easy is essential to Metropolis' growth and prosperity.

## Status

- **Period:** Dec-22 (2022-12-31)
- **Status:** Caution

### Analysis

Metropolis has done a good job promoting a city that is easy to travel to and is easy to get around. Our traffic congestion is very manageable and our airport is operating efficiently.

### Recommendations

We have several new traffic projects we will be undertaking in the next six months. As those projects begin, we need to make sure the detours are effectively designed so we continue to see low levels of traffic congestion.
