## Description

We want our citizens to be active and engaged. Active and engaged citizens make for a more desirable city and the effects will ripple across our town.

## Status

- **Period:** Dec-22 (2022-12-31)
- **Status:** On Target

### Analysis

Like many municipalities across our region, we are not hitting our goals when it comes to citizen engagement. We are seeing steady increases, but we still lag behind where we hope to be.

### Recommendations

We are doing well with encouraging our neighborhood associations to meet more often, but we should continue to check in on this. Hopefully, more active neighborhood associations will lead to more citizen engagement. We&#39;ll be watching that measure closely to see if we see a correlation between improvement in other engagement measures.
