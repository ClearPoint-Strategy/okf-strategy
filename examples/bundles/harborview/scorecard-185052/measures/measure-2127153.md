## Description

Average score residents give the City on the annual community survey, on a 1 to 5 scale. It is Harborview's broadest read on whether services, communication, and responsiveness are meeting expectations; the target is 4.0 or higher.

## Status

- **Period:** Sep-26 (2026-09-30)
- **Status:** Caution
- **Value:** 3.98 (target 4)

### Analysis

September satisfaction is 3.98, up from 3.94 in June but still below the 4.00 target. Survey fielding has closed; the November Council briefing is the opportunity to explain the result without treating a small improvement as target attainment.

### Recommendations

Validate the respondent mix and bring the survey findings and practical service improvements to the Council briefing.
