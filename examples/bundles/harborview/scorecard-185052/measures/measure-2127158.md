## Description

Unrestricted General Fund reserves as a percentage of annual operating expenditures. Healthy reserves protect services during emergencies and revenue downturns and support the City's AA credit rating; the target is 16% or higher.

## Status

- **Period:** Sep-26 (2026-09-30)
- **Status:** On Target
- **Value:** 18.3 (target 17)

### Analysis

Reserves are 18.3%, down from 18.6% but still above the 17% target. A favorable reserve position should not be presented as committed funding for delayed capital work.

### Recommendations

Maintain the reserve floor and distinguish approved capital allocations from potential grant funding in the briefing book.
