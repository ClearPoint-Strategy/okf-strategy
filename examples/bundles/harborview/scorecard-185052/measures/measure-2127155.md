## Description

Share of resident service requests submitted through Harborview Connect and the Permit Center that are resolved within the City's response standard. It shows how reliably day-to-day issues get fixed; the target is 90% or higher.

## Status

- **Period:** Sep-26 (2026-09-30)
- **Status:** On Target
- **Value:** 91.8 (target 90)

### Analysis

The resolution rate improved from 91.2% to 91.8%, remaining above the 90% target. This is a favorable service outcome, not evidence that every strategic project is on schedule.

### Recommendations

Keep the month-end queue review and flag aging requests separately from closed requests.
