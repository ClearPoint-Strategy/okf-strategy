## Description

Affordable housing units delivered through new construction, rehabilitation, and preservation as a percentage of the City's annual production target. It shows whether housing commitments are being met on schedule; the target is 100%.

## Status

- **Period:** Sep-26 (2026-09-30)
- **Status:** On Target
- **Value:** 102.1 (target 100)

### Analysis

Delivery is 102.1% of the annual target, down from 104.3% but still above 100%. This favorable output sits alongside worsening rent burden and delayed trust-fund loan closings.

### Recommendations

Reconcile completed units and their component categories before publication, and distinguish delivered homes from awarded projects awaiting closing.
