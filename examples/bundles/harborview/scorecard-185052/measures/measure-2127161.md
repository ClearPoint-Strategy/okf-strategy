## Description

Number of people experiencing homelessness counted during the point-in-time census, broken out by unsheltered, emergency shelter, and transitional housing. It measures the reach of the City's housing and outreach programs; the target is 2,000 or fewer.

## Status

- **Period:** Sep-26 (2026-09-30)

- **Value:** 1276 (target 1150)

### Analysis

The unsheltered component increased from 1,268 to 1,276, above its lower-is-better target of 1,150. Emergency shelter and transitional housing are reported separately; none of these entries establishes a reduction in unsheltered need.

### Recommendations

Continue zone reconciliation and review placements, inflow and repeat contacts before describing a change in the broader point-in-time count.
