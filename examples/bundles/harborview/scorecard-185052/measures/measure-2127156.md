- **Reporting frequency:** Quarterly
- **Tags:** Community Dashboard, FA: Service Excellence

## Description

Share of eligible City transactions completed online rather than on paper or in person, including permits, business renewals, and program registration. Higher adoption means faster service and lower cost; the target is 60% or higher.

## Status

- **Period:** Sep-26 (2026-09-30)
- **Status:** Below Plan
- **Value:** 50.3 (target 55)

### Analysis

Digital adoption rose from 47.9% to 50.3%, but remains 4.7 percentage points below the September target of 55%. Online permitting went live in September; that milestone does not by itself establish that residents have switched channels.

### Recommendations

Review transactions by service type and focus assisted digital conversion on the remaining paper-heavy workflows.
