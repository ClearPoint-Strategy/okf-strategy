### Analysis

Affordable unit delivery remains above its annual target, but rent burden rose to 52.3% and the unsheltered component rose to 1,276. Trust-fund loan closings have slipped; awards should not be counted as occupied homes.

### Recommendations

Resolve closing dependencies, track placements and preservation, and report the unfavorable household outcomes beside delivered units.
