## Description

Roughly 30% of the distribution network is cast iron installed between 1961 and 1974. Break rates on that cohort are rising, and the late-July Maple St main failure showed how one break can push service request volumes and SLA performance off track citywide.

## Details

- **Approach:** Reduce
- **Impacts:** Unplanned outages and boil-water notices for affected customers Service request surges (412 in nine days after Maple St) that push SLA performance below target Emergency repair costs that displace planned capital work Road and property damage claims
- **Mitigation Strategies:** Accelerate Pipeline Renewal Phase 3 and resequence Phase 2 toward the Maple St failure segment Acoustic leak detection sensors on the highest-risk mains Pre-positioned emergency repair contracts Cohort-based condition scoring feeding the FY27 CIP
