## Description

Replacing the private side of a lead service line requires a signed right-of-entry agreement. Only 38% of affected owners have signed, mostly because of landlord non-response.

## Details

- **Approach:** Reduce
- **Impacts:** Year 1 replacement target (800 lines) missed Risk of non-compliance with federal Lead and Copper Rule deadlines Partial replacements that can temporarily raise lead levels
- **Mitigation Strategies:** Door-to-door outreach with community partners Pursue a landlord consent ordinance Waive all property-owner costs Prioritize schools, daycares and consenting blocks
- **COSO ERM Risk Management:** transfer

## Status

- **Period:** Sep-26 (2026-09-30)
- **Status:** Caution
- **Value:** 12

### Analysis

Rated 12 (medium). Consent went from 31% to 38% this quarter after door-to-door outreach started. Council will consider a landlord consent ordinance in December.
