## Description

Municipal governments are frequent ransomware targets. A successful attack could take down permitting, utility billing, 911 CAD integrations and the ERP.

## Details

- **Approach:** Reduce,Transfer
- **Impacts:** Loss of online resident services and utility billing for days or weeks Exposure of resident personal data Recovery costs and potential ransom demands Reputational damage and loss of public trust
- **Mitigation Strategies:** Multi-factor authentication on all staff accounts (complete) Immutable offsite backups tested quarterly Annual phishing training and simulated campaigns Cyber liability insurance policy ($5M) 24x7 managed detection and response contract
- **COSO ERM Risk Management:** mitigate
