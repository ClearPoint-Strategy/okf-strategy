### Analysis

Available figures put budget variance at 2.6%, within its 3% ceiling, and secured grants at $3,553K FYTD, above target. Both are carried forward from June. The narrow variance margin and unreconciled capital schedules warrant caution even though the recorded measures meet target.

### Recommendations

Reconcile departmental requests and capital timing for the September close. In the council dashboard, place a confirmed, funded priority beside any proposal that remains contingent on external funding; do not budget proposed federal funding as an award.
