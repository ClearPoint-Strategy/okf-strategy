### Analysis

SLA closure finished the quarter at 88.6% against a 90% target — the first miss in eight quarters. The shortfall is concentrated in the Maple St corridor, where the water main incident in late July generated 412 unplanned service requests in nine days. Those tickets carried a median age of 6.1 days against a 3.4-day baseline, and they alone account for roughly 1.8 points of the gap. Excluding the incident window, closure held at 91.2%.

### Recommendations

No structural change recommended. The corridor backlog cleared on 8/29 and weekly closure returned above 91% for the final three weeks of the quarter. Recommend holding the 90% target for Q4-26 and adding a corridor-level breakout to the Public Works performance report so incident-driven spikes are visible before they show up in the quarterly roll-up.
