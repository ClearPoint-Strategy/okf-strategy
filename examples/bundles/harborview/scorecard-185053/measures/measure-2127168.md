### Analysis

Average first response held at 3.9 hours against a 4.0-hour target. Response time absorbed the Maple St incident volume better than closure did — the after-hours dispatch rotation added in Q1-26 kept intake within target even at peak load on 7/28–7/31, when daily request volume ran 4.3x normal.

### Recommendations

Keep the after-hours rotation funded through Q4-26. It is the main reason response time stayed green while SLA closure slipped, and it is the cheaper of the two levers available for the winter main-break season.
