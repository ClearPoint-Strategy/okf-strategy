### Analysis

Library visits per capita narrowly exceeded target at 0.46 against 0.45, while after-school capacity was 81.4% against 85% and cost recovery was 37.3% against 40%. Riverside branch renovation remains at 70% and is past its October 9 end date. Residents can use existing services, but the unfinished branch work must not be described as a completed access improvement.
