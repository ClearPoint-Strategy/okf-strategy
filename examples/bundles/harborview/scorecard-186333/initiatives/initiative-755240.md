### Analysis

Q3 actions: 17 schools now run a weekend pack, care closet or share table, up from 12 in Q2, with the back-to-school launch adding five. Growth slowed because some schools lack volunteer coordinators and cold storage. Partners: Harborview Unified School District, Harbor Area Food Bank, United Way of the Harbor Region.

### Recommendations

Next quarter: Fund refrigerators for four schools, appoint a parent coordinator at each new site, and aim for 22 schools by year end.
