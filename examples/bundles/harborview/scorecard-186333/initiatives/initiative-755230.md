### Analysis

Q3 actions: Added a weekly Bayfront lunch table and two Riverside porch socials, bringing quarterly events to 26. Moved the Baby Cafe to evening hours in July, and Family Home Visiting held its third small-group series. Library branches now post an events board. Partners: Harborview Community Health Center, Parks/Rec/Libraries, United Way of the Harbor Region.
