### Analysis

Q3 actions: Brought the directory to 49 free and low-cost food sites by adding a Bayshore pantry, a Seagrove mobile stop and a Riverside produce table. Surveys now show 14.6% of adults worried about running out of food, down from 15.8% in Q1. Partners: Harbor Area Food Bank, Harborview Housing Authority, faith partners.

### Recommendations

Next quarter: Map walking distance to sites and place one new distribution in Dune Avenue, the longest gap, before the holidays.
