### Analysis

Q3 actions: Held 14 listening sessions with 210 residents and gathered 160 short surveys. The top barriers were cost, work schedules, and fear of judgment, and clinics responded by adding two evening walk-in hours. Partners: Coastal Behavioral Health, United Way of the Harbor Region, faith partners.

### Recommendations

Next quarter: Publish a plain-language What We Heard report and fund a transportation option in the neighborhoods that raised it.
