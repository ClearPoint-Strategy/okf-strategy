### Analysis

Q3 actions: Screened and referred 462 residents, up 35% from Q2, after adding the screening questions to WIC and clinic visits. Started a warm-handoff line staffed by Public Health nurses who schedule the first appointment before the resident leaves. Partners: Harborview Community Health Center, Coastal Behavioral Health, Bayview Medical Center.

### Recommendations

Next quarter: Track how many referrals result in a completed first visit, and extend warm handoffs to the Bayfront school-based health center.
