### Analysis

Q3 actions: Peak season brought two new community garden plots in Riverside and East Flats, a Saturday farm stand at the Bayfront library, and matching dollars at the farmers market for residents on food assistance. Harvest donations to pantries rose steadily across the summer. Partners: Parks/Rec/Libraries, Harbor Area Food Bank, Harborview Community Health Center.

### Recommendations

Next quarter: Move the matching program indoors with a winter market and plan fall planting of cold-hardy greens at both new gardens.
