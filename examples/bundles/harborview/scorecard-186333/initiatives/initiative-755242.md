### Analysis

Q3 actions: Mailed a benefits guide to 9,000 households and placed enrollment staff at two farmers markets and the Harbor Creek library. The directory page for food help was the most visited section, and call-center staff saw more first-time callers. Partners: Harborview Housing Authority, Harbor Area Food Bank, Parks/Rec/Libraries.

### Recommendations

Next quarter: Translate the guide into Spanish and Haitian Creole, and embed a food screener at school registration.
