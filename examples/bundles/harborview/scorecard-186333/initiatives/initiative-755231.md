### Analysis

Q3 actions: Outreach staff made 231 targeted contacts, up from 164 in Q2, concentrating on Old Ferry Road senior housing and seaport night-shift workers. Launched a friendly-caller list of 40 volunteers and began a short isolation check-in at every home visit. Partners: Harborview Housing Authority, Seaport Workers Clinic, faith partners.

### Recommendations

Next quarter: Train the friendly-caller volunteers on referral steps, and add Seagrove senior buildings to the outreach route before winter.
