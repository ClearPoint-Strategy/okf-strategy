### Analysis

Training volume is strong, with 214 people trained in Q3 and a consistent 23-point knowledge gain, and the coalition has grown to 17 partners. Two gaps keep this goal at Caution: only 7 coalition partners serve higher-risk groups, and stigma messaging has not yet reached the Seagrove and East Flats audiences we promised. We will not call the goal on target until both close.

### Recommendations

Close the higher-risk partner gap and take stigma messaging to East Flats and Seagrove.
