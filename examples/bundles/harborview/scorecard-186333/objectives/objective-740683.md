### Analysis

Classroom prevention lessons and after-school programs are both on pace to finish the year as planned, and prevention materials are now in the hands of families at every middle school. The school discipline alternatives strategy is the one lagging piece, because only a few campuses have adopted the new response model. Policy review work will inform whether this needs a formal district action.

### Recommendations

Secure a district liaison for discipline alternatives and finish the policy audit.
