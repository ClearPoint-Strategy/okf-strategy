### Analysis

The resource directory lists 49 free and low-cost food sites, up from 41 in Q1, and 17 schools now run weekend packs, care closets or share tables. The share of adults worried about running out of food dipped from 15.8% to 14.6% over the baseline year. Dune Avenue and Seagrove remain the longest walks to a site and drive the Q4 siting plan.

### Recommendations

Site a new distribution in Dune Avenue and fund cold storage for the remaining schools.
