### Analysis

Social connection programming nearly doubled its reach this year, with 26 events and programs and 905 attendees in Q3 against 14 and 420 in Q1. The new-parent groups, isolation outreach and 988 awareness strategies are reinforcing each other, since outreach staff now hand residents a resource-directory card at every contact. The remaining work is to reach residents who do not come to events at all.

### Recommendations

Extend programming through winter with indoor venues and track repeat attendance.
