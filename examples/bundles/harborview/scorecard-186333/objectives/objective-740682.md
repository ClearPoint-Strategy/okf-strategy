### Analysis

Summer was the strongest stretch for produce access, with garden harvests, farm stands and matching-dollar markets all operating at full capacity. Nutrition education is reaching families through schools, libraries and neighborhood events. The challenge now is carrying that momentum through the winter months when outdoor sites close.

### Recommendations

Move the matching-dollar program indoors for winter and expand cooking demonstrations.
