### Analysis

Screening-and-referral volume rose from 238 in Q1 to 462 in Q3, and 118 City and partner staff have completed mental health literacy training. The coordination agreement now has 15 signers. The agreement still needs more signatories who work directly with higher-risk groups, which is the main item for Q4.
