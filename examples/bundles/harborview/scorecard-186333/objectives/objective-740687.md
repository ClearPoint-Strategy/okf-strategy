### Analysis

Screening events and vaccination clinics expanded steadily through the summer, and the fall respiratory season clinics are already on the calendar. Partnering with faith communities and employers has made it easier to reach adults who do not have a regular clinic. The next step is tracking follow-up care so a positive screening leads to a visit.

### Recommendations

Track follow-up care after screenings and complete the fall clinic calendar.
