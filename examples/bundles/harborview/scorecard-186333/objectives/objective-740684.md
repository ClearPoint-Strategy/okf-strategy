### Analysis

Public education sessions, materials placement and naloxone training all moved forward in Q3, helped by the late-summer overdose awareness campaign. Employer and faith partner outreach is progressing more slowly than the rest. Resident feedback on barriers to treatment is now shaping where we place materials and which hours clinics offer walk-in access.

### Recommendations

Recruit employers with shift-friendly formats and place public naloxone cabinets.
