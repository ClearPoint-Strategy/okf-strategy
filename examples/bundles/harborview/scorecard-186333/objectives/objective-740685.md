### Analysis

Navigation workshops, age-tailored health literacy sessions and a combined care listing all progressed in Q3. Seaport Workers Clinic and Harborview Community Health Center are co-hosting the sessions that draw the most attendees. Spanish and Tagalog versions of the care listing are the main gap to close before year end.

### Recommendations

Add Spanish and Tagalog care listings and train navigators for open enrollment.
