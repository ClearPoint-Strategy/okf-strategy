## Description

Jet fuel is one of the largest operating expenses for airlines, and fluctuations in fuel prices can lead to unpredictable cost structures. Rapid increases in fuel prices can erode profit margins, making it difficult to achieve financial targets and deliver consistent returns to shareholders. Conversely, while lower fuel prices can benefit the airline in the short term, they can also signal broader economic instability, which could affect demand for air travel.
