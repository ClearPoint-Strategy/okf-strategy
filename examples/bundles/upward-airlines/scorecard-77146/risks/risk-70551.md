## Description

This risk focuses on operational disruptions that can impede our objective of ensuring frequent, reliable departures. Such disruptions can arise from a variety of sources, including adverse weather conditions, technical malfunctions, air traffic control issues, crew shortages, and logistical challenges. Frequent delays and cancellations not only disrupt schedules but also damage the airline's reputation, leading to decreased customer satisfaction and loyalty.
