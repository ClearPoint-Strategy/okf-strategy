### Analysis

As of Q2, the initiative is progressing as planned and is currently 50% complete. Key progress this quarter includes: 
 
 
 Ancillary Services Expansion: Upward Airlines successfully launched a premium in-flight Wi-Fi package and redesigned its &agrave; la carte menu to include high-margin, locally sourced items on long-haul flights. These changes have already generated a 12% increase in per-passenger ancillary revenue compared to Q1. 
 
 
 Loyalty Program Monetization: A partnership agreement was signed with two major hotel chains and a credit card provider, allowing customers to earn and redeem miles across a broader network. Early engagement data suggests a strong uptick in new loyalty program sign-ups (up 18% MoM). 
 
 
 Cargo Services Pilot: A pilot program for regional cargo transport was launched in the Midwest, repurposing underutilized aircraft capacity on less-than-full routes. While still in early stages, preliminary results are promising with average load rates hitting 65% of goal.
