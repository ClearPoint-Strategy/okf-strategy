## Description

We need to be engaged with our employees as Satisfaction and Turnover are closely correlated. Our current survey wasn't giving us the information we needed to stay on top of our employee happiness. We are redesigning the satisfaction survey with the hopes of staying on top of the pulse of the organization and reacting faster to opportunities given by our employees to get better.

## Status — Dec-25 (Above Target)

**Analysis.** As of Q2, the initiative is progressing as planned and is currently 50% complete. Key progress this quarter includes: Ancillary Services Expansion: Upward Airlines successfully launched a premium in-flight Wi-Fi package and redesigned its &agrave; la carte menu to include high-margin, locally sourced items on long-haul flights. These changes have already generated a 12% increase in per-passenger ancillary revenue compared to Q1. Loyalty Program Monetization: A partnership agreement was signed with two major hotel chains and a credit card provider, allowing customers to earn and redeem miles across a broader network. Early engagement data suggests a strong uptick in new loyalty program sign-ups (up 18% MoM). Cargo Services Pilot: A pilot program for regional cargo transport was launched in the Midwest, repurposing underutilized aircraft capacity on less-than-full routes. While still in early stages, preliminary results are promising with average load rates hitting 65% of goal.

**Recommendations.** It will make sense to invest a little more now so as not to miss the timing during our corporate calendar.
