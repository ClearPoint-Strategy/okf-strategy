## Description

We need to be engaged with our employees as Satisfaction and Turnover are closely correlated. Our current survey wasn't giving us the information we needed to stay on top of our employee happiness. We are redesigning the satisfaction survey with the hopes of staying on top of the pulse of the organization and reacting faster to opportunities given by our employees to get better.
